# Newcastle LGBTQ+ Voice

Editorial language for reporting on the Newcastle LGBTQ+ community.

## Language

### News publishing

**Published article**:
A news article with no draft flag whose publication date is on or before today in Pacific time.
_Avoid_: Live, visible or public article, when the date or draft state is unspecified

### Election reporting

**Reporting snapshot**:
A dated account of the election information verified by a stated cutoff, with the underlying results' own update times distinguished from that cutoff.
_Avoid_: Live results, when the account is not continuously maintained

**Current leader**:
The candidate or ballot response with the most votes in the reported count, without an implied prediction of the final outcome.
_Avoid_: Winner, elected, approved, rejected as an outcome solely on the basis of a lead

**Attributed race call**:
A named news organization's determination of a race's winner, distinct from election officials' certification.
_Avoid_: Certified result, official winner when only a media call is established

**Certified result**:
An election outcome formally certified by the responsible election authority.
_Avoid_: Final result for an uncertified count

**If-leads-hold composition**:
The hypothetical partisan makeup if every current leader wins, including any seats not up for election.
_Avoid_: Secured majority, seats won when the calculation includes uncalled leads

**Secured seats**:
Seats accounted for by verified race calls or certified results, together with continuing seats not up for election when describing a whole chamber.
_Avoid_: Mixing current leads into seats secured
