:Dateline Six Washington PFLAG chapters have joined a growing coalition of more than 80 distinguished state organizations in opposition to two anti-LGBTQ ballot initiatives scheduled to appear on the November 2026 ballot. The initiatives, backed by Redmond-based hedge fund manager Brian Heywood's Let's Go Washington committee, target LGBTQ+ students in Washington's public schools.

---

## The Initiatives

Two measures are on the November ballot:

- **IL26-001**, referred to by opponents as the "Repeal of the Safety Act," would eliminate existing student safety protections that currently shield LGBTQ+ youth in Washington schools.
- **IL26-638**, which opponents have named "Putting Girls At Risk," would require every girl who competes in girls' interschool sports to have a health care provider verify her sex based on reproductive anatomy, genetic makeup, or testosterone levels. Opponents warn that this could involve invasive examinations; the text does not say which method a provider must use.

Both initiatives were sponsored by Let's Go Washington, a Redmond-based political committee led by hedge fund manager Brian Heywood, and qualified for the ballot by law.

---

## What They Would Do

Taken together, the two initiatives would produce three specific and concrete harms:

**Put LGBTQ+ youth at risk of being outed.** IL26-001 does not say schools must notify parents because a student is LGBTQ+. It would, however, widen the records schools must give parents to any student-specific file, require them within 10 business days, and require notice if a child is removed from campus, including to a youth shelter or host home. Opponents argue this exposes LGBTQ+ students to unsupportive families, and research they cite, including from the Trevor Project, links family rejection of LGBTQ+ youth to higher rates of homelessness and mental health crises.

**Restrict student access to support.** Students who are experiencing domestic violence or sexual abuse at home could face new barriers to confiding in school counselors. IL26-001 would restore an explicit parental right to inspect counseling records, and would shield only medical, health, and mental health counseling records, and only while an abuse or neglect investigation of the parent is pending.

**Add a medical verification requirement for girls only.** Girls who want to play on interschool teams, which can include middle school, would need a provider's signed verification of their sex, and opponents warn that this could involve invasive examinations. The text creates no matching requirement for boys' teams.

---

## Who Is Opposing Them

Six Washington PFLAG chapters have formally joined the coalition opposing both initiatives:

- **PFLAG Bellevue/Eastside**
- **PFLAG Seattle**
- **PFLAG Wenatchee/Wenatchee Valley**
- **PFLAG Olympia**
- **PFLAG Skagit**
- **PFLAG Bellingham/Whatcom County**

They stand alongside more than 80 other distinguished state organizations that have signed on in opposition. Washington has a long history of defeating attacks on civil rights at the ballot box, and advocates are confident that history can repeat itself here.

## How to Take Action

**Contact your legislators.** Call or submit a written comment at [leg.wa.gov/help/contact-us](https://leg.wa.gov/help/contact-us/). If you call, you will typically reach voicemail, identify yourself as a constituent, state that you oppose these initiatives, and keep your message brief so staffers can tally responses efficiently. A written comment form is also available; it looks up your legislators by address and allows you to copy additional representatives.

**Learn more and get involved.** Full action resources, including a template for contacting newspapers and legislators prepared by **Gender Justice League**, are available at [nohateinwastate.org](https://nohateinwastate.org/).
