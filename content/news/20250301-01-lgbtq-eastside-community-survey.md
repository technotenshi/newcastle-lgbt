**Newcastle, WA** – Eastside Pride PNW is working to foster unity, celebrate diversity, and build bridges across communities. To ensure their work reflects the needs of the local LGBTQIA2+ community, they are gathering input from community members like you.

I’m encouraging you to take a few minutes to complete their **anonymous community survey**. This is your opportunity to share what matters most to you, what challenges you face, and how Eastside Pride PNW can better serve and support our LGBTQIA2+ community. 

[Click here to take the survey now!](https://forms.office.com/r/W9r5T0RByr)

### Why Your Input Matters
Every community member has unique experiences, concerns, and ideas. Whether it’s advocating for better legal protections, organizing social events, or expanding access to inclusive healthcare, your feedback will help shape the future of LGBTQIA2+ support on the Eastside.

By sharing your thoughts, you’ll help ensure that Eastside Pride PNW remains a strong, supportive, and responsive organization. **All responses are confidential, and every question is optional.**

### What the Survey Covers
The survey asks about:
- The biggest issues facing the LGBTQIA2+ community on the Eastside
- The types of events and programs you’d like to see
- The work you think Eastside Pride PNW should focus on
- How you prefer to stay connected with LGBTQIA2+ advocacy efforts
- Opportunities to get involved through volunteering or sponsorship

### Make Your Voice Heard
This is a chance to have a direct impact on the future of LGBTQIA2+ advocacy on the Eastside. Whether you’re passionate about activism, community-building, or just want to meet more people in a welcoming space, your voice matters.

Take the survey today and help shape the future of LGBTQIA2+ advocacy in our community!

[Take the survey now!](https://forms.office.com/r/W9r5T0RByr)
