The **Washington State LGBTQ Commission** is convening its March public meeting in Seattle, focused on the state's response to 2SLGBTQIA+ housing concerns. Attend in person or join online to hear from state agency leadership and staff, and to share public comment.

The meeting spans two days:

- **Friday, March 27, 2026, 12:00–5:00 PM**
- **Saturday, March 28, 2026, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM**

**In person:** GSBA Office, 400 E Pine St, Ste 322, Seattle, WA 98122

**Online:** Join via Zoom at [ofm-wa-gov.zoom.us/j/84294795412](https://ofm-wa-gov.zoom.us/j/84294795412), Meeting ID: 842-9479-5412

The public is welcome to observe any portion of the meeting. **Public comment begins at 11:30 AM on Saturday** and is open to all 2SLGBTQIA+ individuals who wish to share their experiences with the Commission, in person or virtually.

Thank you to **GSBA**, Washington State's LGBTQ+ and allied chamber of commerce, for hosting. Learn more at [lgbtq.wa.gov](https://lgbtq.wa.gov/).
