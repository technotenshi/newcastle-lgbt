GenPride Seattle hosts Silver in the Rainbow, a Halloween dance party for LGBTQ+ elders ages 50 and up, with music from DJ Mixx America and DJ Dunye and soul line dancing that beginners can join. It runs Saturday, October 31, 2026, from 6:00–10:00 PM at Russian Hall, 704 19th Ave E in Seattle, with appetizers, beer, and wine included with your ticket. A costume contest awards $150 for best couple, $100 for best solo, and two $50 honorable mentions, and scholarship tickets are available for anyone who needs one. Get tickets using the link below.
