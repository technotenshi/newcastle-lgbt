Join us on **Saturday, November 16, 12:00–3:00 p.m.** at **Blakely Hall** in Issaquah for the **Rainbow Connections Intergenerational Pride Event**! This vibrant LGBTQIA+ celebration will bridge the gap between generations, honoring the wisdom of the past, the energy of the present, and the vision for the future.

This free event will feature Community mosaic project, Remembrance Wall, Resource fair, Catered food from queer-owned businesses, Friendship bracelet making, Games, activities, and crafts, and Discussion panel with queer community representatives.

Don’t miss out on this exciting opportunity to **connect, celebrate diversity, and strengthen bonds** across all ages!
