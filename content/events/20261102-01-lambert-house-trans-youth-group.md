Lambert House's Trans Youth Group meets every Monday for transgender and gender questioning youth, in person at the Capitol Hill Drop-In Center and online on Zoom in a hybrid format. The next meeting is Monday, November 2, 2026, from 7:00–8:30 PM at 1229 10th Ave E in Seattle. A New Youth Orientation is required for first-time participants, so email OnlineGroups@LambertHouse.org to sign up, and see the link below for details.
