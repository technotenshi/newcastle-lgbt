Lambert House hosts an online Dungeons & Dragons one-shot campaign for LGBTQ+ youth, run on Zoom by employees of Wizards of the Coast, the creators of D&D. The session is Monday, November 16, 2026, from 5:00–7:00 PM, and these campaigns usually meet on the fourth Monday of the month. A New Youth Orientation is required for first-time participants, so email OnlineGroups@LambertHouse.org to sign up and get the link, and see the link below for details.
