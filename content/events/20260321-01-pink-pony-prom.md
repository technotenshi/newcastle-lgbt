**Bellevue College's Queer & Trans Resource Center** is throwing the **Pink Pony Prom**, a judgment-free queer prom where everyone is welcome to celebrate freely, dress how they want, and dance the afternoon away.

Join us on **Saturday, March 21, 2026, from 3:00–6:00 PM** at **Bellevue College, U Building (Student Success Center), 3rd Floor, Room U-301 Ballroom**.

This event is open to everyone, Bellevue College students, faculty, and staff; local high schoolers; and all Eastside community members. Whether you never got to attend prom the way you wanted or just want to add to the festive atmosphere, you are welcome here.

The event features food and beverages, a live DJ, and performances by local drag artists. Attendance is **free**. If you choose, you can make an optional $10 donation at registration to support future LGBTQ+ events and resources at Bellevue College.

**Registration is required.** Secure your spot at [tinyurl.com/PinkPonyProm](https://tinyurl.com/PinkPonyProm). For questions, contact the Queer & Trans Resource Center at lgbtq@bellevuecollege.edu or visit [bellevuecollege.edu/mcs/lgbtq](https://www.bellevuecollege.edu/mcs/lgbtq).
