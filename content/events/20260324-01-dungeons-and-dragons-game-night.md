**Join us for a D&D Game Night at the Newcastle Library,** where a Dungeon Master will introduce participants to Dungeons and Dragons and set up campaign play.

All materials are provided, including books, maps, miniatures, dice, and character sheets, so no prior experience is required.

Join us on **Tuesday, March 24, 2026, from 6:00–7:45 PM.** This event is recommended for teens, tweens, and young adults ages 12–24.

Space is limited, so register online in advance. Sponsored by the Friends of the Newcastle Library.
