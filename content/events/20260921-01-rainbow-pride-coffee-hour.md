Rainbow Pride Coffee Hour is a free monthly conversation and coffee hour at the Peter Kirk Community Center dedicated to building connections for LGBTQIA+ older adults and their allies. The next gathering is Monday, September 21, 2026, from 11:00 AM–12:00 PM, and it repeats on the third Monday of every month. The hour is facilitated by Cassidy Stout, MSW, a Community Resource Specialist, and works to improve social inclusion and strengthen services for LGBTQIA+ seniors. Learn more using the link below.
