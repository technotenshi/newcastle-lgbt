Lambert House's 10-14 Year Old Group is a 10-week cohort for LGBTQ+ youth ages 10 to 14 focused on having fun and making friends, with activities such as baking, show and tell, and arts and crafts. This is not a drop-in group: the fall cohort is already enrolled and meets Saturdays from 3:00–4:30 PM through November 21, with the October 31 session on Zoom. Registration for future cohorts is by email, with a sliding scale fee of $100–$250 and scholarships available; see the link below.
