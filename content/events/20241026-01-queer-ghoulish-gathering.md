Join the **Queer Ghoulish Gathering** for a fun and spooky day at Lake Sammamish State Park! LGBTQIA+ youth ages 16 to 22 are invited to take part in a hike/walk, treasure hunt, games, and a creative costume contest. Fabulous food and prizes will also be part of the fun.

The event will take place on Saturday, October 26, from 10 am to 12 pm. Be sure to register on the Friends of Lake Sammamish State Park website to secure your spot!
