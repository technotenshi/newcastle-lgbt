**Cars on the Green Auto Show**, part of Newcastle Days, is a free showcase of classic cruisers, muscle cars, and custom rides on **Saturday, September 19, 2026, from 10:00 AM–3:00 PM** at **Lake Boren Park (alternate entrance), 7840 129th Ave SE, Newcastle, WA 98056**.

Parking gates open at 8 AM and the show opens to the public at 10 AM, with Newcastle Days festivities, food vendors, and stage entertainment joining at 11 AM. People's Choice and participant judging closes at 1:30 PM, followed by an awards ceremony at 2:30 PM. Registration is free, courtesy of sponsor The Garage in Renton, and must be completed online by 5:00 PM on Wednesday, September 16. Visit the link below for more information.
