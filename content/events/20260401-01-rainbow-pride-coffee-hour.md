**Rainbow Pride Coffee Hour** meets on the **first Wednesday of every month from 9:00–10:00 AM** at the **Peter Kirk Community Center, 352 Kirkland Ave, Kirkland, WA**. This month's gathering is on **Wednesday, April 1, 2026**.

Facilitated by **Cassidy Stout**, this free monthly hour is dedicated to building connections for LGBTQIA+ older adults and their allies, fostering social inclusion and helping evolve services to better meet the needs of LGBTQIA+ seniors in our community.

Attendance is **free**. Email cassidys@mynorthshore.org for details.
