Join us on **Sunday, June 8th, 2025, from 3–6 PM** at **Lake Boren Park in Newcastle** to celebrate **Newcastle’s Pride in the Park**!

This family-friendly community event honors the **LGBTQIA+ community** with joy, connection, and visibility in the heart of Newcastle. Come out for a colorful afternoon filled with celebration, unity, and pride.

**Location:**  
Lake Boren Park  
Newcastle, WA
