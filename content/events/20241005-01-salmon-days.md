The 55th Annual Salmon Days Festival is back in Issaquah! Enjoy a flippin' fantastic weekend filled with fun activities, local vendors, and entertainment for everyone. From the iconic Salmon Days Grande Parade to exploring the festival grounds, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.

The parade kicks off at 10 AM on Saturday, starting at Confluence Park and weaving through the streets of Issaquah. Bring your family, friends, and a sense of adventure to celebrate this longstanding tradition.
