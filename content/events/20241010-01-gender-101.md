Join Plateaupians for Peace for **Gender 101**, a virtual educational event that offers an introduction to understanding gender identity and expression. This event is designed for anyone looking to learn more about gender diversity in an open, respectful, and welcoming space.

The event takes place via Zoom, providing an accessible platform for participants to engage with the material and ask questions. Don’t miss this opportunity to gain a deeper understanding of gender issues in a supportive environment. Registration is required to attend.
