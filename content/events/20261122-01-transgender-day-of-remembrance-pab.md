Pride Across the Bridge invites the community to a gentle, cozy afternoon to remember the transgender and nonbinary people lost this year, with space to reflect, hold their loved ones close, and sing together. It takes place Sunday, November 22, 2026, from 3:00–5:00 PM at the Kirkland Woman's Club, 407 First Street, a non-faith space the organization has reserved for the entire building. Bring your own blanket and pillow, and light refreshments will be provided. Please RSVP using the link below.
