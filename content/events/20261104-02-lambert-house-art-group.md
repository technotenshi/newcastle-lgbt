Lambert House's Art Group meets every Wednesday in person at the Capitol Hill Drop-In Center for LGBTQ+ youth to make art, talk about art, and express themselves, with no set curriculum or expectations. The next session is Wednesday, November 4, 2026, from 7:00–8:00 PM at 1229 10th Ave E in Seattle. A New Youth Orientation is required for first-time participants; see the link below for details.
