**Pinch Pot Pride Hand Building Workshop** is a family-friendly clay class where you'll create ceramic pieces by hand and decorate them with the pride colors of your choosing. The workshop runs on **Tuesday, June 2, 2026, from 4:00–5:30 PM** at the **Northwest Pottery Collective, 1445 Northwest Mall Street, Issaquah, WA**.

No experience is necessary and the class is open to ages 16 and up. Children ages 5–15 are welcome to enroll alongside an enrolled adult. Register via the link above to reserve your spot.
