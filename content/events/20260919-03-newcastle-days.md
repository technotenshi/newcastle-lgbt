**Newcastle Days**, the community's signature celebration, takes place on **Saturday, September 19, 2026, from 10:00 AM–7:00 PM** at **Lake Boren Park, 13058 SE 84th Way, Newcastle, WA 98056**.

This free festival, held each September to honor Newcastle's 1994 cityhood, features the Cars on the Green auto show, a parade and opening ceremonies led by bagpiper Neil Hubbard, live stage entertainment, community booths, carnival games, giant inflatables, a craft beer and wine garden, and food vendors throughout the day.
