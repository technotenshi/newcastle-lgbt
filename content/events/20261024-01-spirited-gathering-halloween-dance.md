Eastside Pride PNW hosts its first dance party, A Spirited Gathering, an intergenerational LGBTQIA+ and allies Halloween dance with food, socializing, and music from the 70s and 80s. The free, ages-16-and-up event runs Saturday, October 24, 2026, from 6:00–10:00 PM at the Kirkland Teen Union Building, 348 Kirkland Ave, an accessible venue close to transit. Family-friendly costumes and festive attire are optional and encouraged, and pizza and beverages will be provided. Pre-registration is encouraged using the link below, or RSVP by emailing info@eastsidepridepnw.com.
