**Concerts in the Park** wraps its 2026 season with **Glow in the Park Night** featuring the band Free Rain, on **Wednesday, August 12, 2026, from 6:00–8:30 PM** at **Lake Boren Park, 13058 SE 84th Way, Newcastle, WA**.

The park opens at 6 PM with food vendors, family activities, and community booths, followed by live music on stage at 7 PM. Bring your brightest neon and glow sticks for a night of rock anthems, high-voltage dance tracks, and massive sing-alongs. Food vendors include DIY Tea Lab, Gourminis, Taco Cortes, and Tastyz.
