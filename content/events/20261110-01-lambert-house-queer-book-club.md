Lambert House's Queer Book Club is an online group on Zoom for LGBTQ+ youth, meeting on the second Tuesday of each month. The next meeting is Tuesday, November 10, 2026, from 4:00–5:30 PM. A New Youth Orientation is required for first-time participants, so email OnlineGroups@LambertHouse.org to sign up and get the Zoom link, and see the link below for details.
