Join Governor Ferguson, the Washington State LGBTQ Commission, and the Rainbow Alliance and Inclusion Network as they raise the intersex-inclusive progress pride flag at the Capitol Campus to welcome in pride season.

The ceremony takes place on **Wednesday, June 3, 2026, at 12:00 PM** at the Washington State Capitol Campus, 416 Sid Snyder Avenue SW, Olympia, WA -- the flag raising will be held at the flag circle between the Legislative Building and the Temple of Justice.

Hear from various 2SLGBTQIA+ and allied leaders across state government. Can't make it in person? Watch live or later on TVW.

For more information, visit the Washington State LGBTQ Commission at lgbtq.wa.gov.
