The **QTBIPOC Arts Fest**, hosted by the Transgender Health and Wellness Center of Washington (Trans-Wa) with Seattle Parks & Recreation and the Seattle Office of Arts & Culture, celebrates creativity and community at **Othello Park in Seattle on Saturday, September 12, 2026, from 12:00–4:00 PM**. The free, all-ages event centers QTBIPOC artists and organizations with craft workshops, an artist and community vendor fair, and an open mic for readings and music, and welcomes people of all genders, ages, and races. An accessibility guide covering transit, parking, and seating is available on the organizer's site, and questions can be sent to jax@transwa.org. For more information and to sign up as an artist, vendor, or volunteer, visit the link below.
