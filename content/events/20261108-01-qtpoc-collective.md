QTPOC Collective is a monthly community gathering from Pride Across the Bridge and Eastside For All for queer and trans people of color. This month's meetup is a Bystander Intervention Workshop on how to safely respond when you witness or are targeted by bias or hate. It runs Sunday, November 8, 2026, from 1:00–3:00 PM at the Together Center in Redmond, with snacks provided. The gathering is for people with lived experience as members of historically minoritized racial groups, and the link below has details on the lounge entrance.
