Due to the Newcastle City Council's recent vote based on intolerance and ignorance, our community has been deeply affected. We want to make it clear that Newcastle stands for inclusivity, safety, and welcome for our LGBTQ+ neighbors. Join us in a peaceful and joyous gathering at **Lake Boren Park on June 18th at 5 PM**, adorned in our most vibrant rainbow colors. Together, we'll celebrate our unity and resilience with inspiring speeches before marching as a proud group to City Hall for the regular council meeting at 7 PM.

We aim to fill the council chamber with the spirit of Pride, encouraging respectful public comments to ensure our voices are heard by those who voted against LGBTQ+ visibility. We invite everyone to bring Pride-related clothing, flags, and items to donate, showing our collective support. For more details, watch KING 5's coverage of the council meeting and hear the statements from Mayor Clark and Councilmember Tallman. Share your thoughts and take action by contacting Newcastle's Council at **[citycouncil@newcastle.gov](mailto:citycouncil@newcastle.gov)**. Let's come together and make a powerful statement for equality and acceptance!
