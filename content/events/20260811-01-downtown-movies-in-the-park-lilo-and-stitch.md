**Downtown Movies in the Park** returns for **Island Vibes Night**, an outdoor screening of *Lilo & Stitch*, on **Tuesday, August 11, 2026, from 7:00–10:00 PM** at **Bellevue Downtown Park, 10201 NE 4th St, Bellevue, WA 98004**.

Entertainment begins at 7 PM and the movie starts at dusk. Eastside Pride will have a community booth at the event, free and open to all, and volunteers are needed. Visit the link below to sign up.
