Eastside Pride PNW hosts Play With Pride, a free, all-ages community game night with board games for every skill level, pizza, snacks, and non-alcoholic beverages. It runs Saturday, November 21, 2026, from 6:00–10:00 PM at Zulu's Guild Hall Prelude, 19111 Bothell Way NE in Bothell. No board game experience is needed, and guests are welcome to bring a favorite game to share. Please RSVP using the link below so organizers can plan food and drinks, and mention if you would like to volunteer to set up, lead games, or clean up.
