Join us for **Pride Night** at Village Theatre’s **Legally Blonde The Musical** on Sunday, November 24, at 7:30 p.m. in Issaquah! Presented in partnership with GSBA, Eastside Pride PNW, and The Garage, this special event is a celebration of community and fabulous fashion.

Enjoy a 25% discount on tickets with the code **PRIDENIGHT** at checkout. Dress in your most stylish pink attire and get ready to pose on the pink carpet with fun props provided. Don’t miss this chance to experience a memorable night of theater and celebration. [Learn more about the show](https://villagetheatre.org/shows/blonde/).
