**Arts in the Park**, hosted by OneRedmond as part of the city's Welcoming Week, returns to **Downtown Park** on **Saturday, September 19, 2026, from 4:00–8:00 PM** with live music and community art activities. The free, family-friendly event celebrates the diverse backgrounds and stories that make Redmond a welcoming community. For more information, visit the link below.
