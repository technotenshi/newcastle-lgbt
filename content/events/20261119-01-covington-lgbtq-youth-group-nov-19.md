Covington LGBTQ+ Youth Group is a monthly in-person peer support group from Lambert House for LGBTQ+ youth ages 10–22, offered at a King County library. It meets Thursday, November 19, 2026, from 4:30–6:00 PM, and repeats on the third Thursday of each month. A New Youth Orientation is required for first-time participants, so email OnlineGroups@LambertHouse.org to sign up and get the exact location, and see the link below for more information.
