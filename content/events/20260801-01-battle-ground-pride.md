**Battle Ground Pride 2026** is a free, all-ages event hosted by Evoke Cascadia in Battle Ground, WA, taking place on **Saturday, August 1, 2026, from 11:00 AM–4:00 PM** at the **Battle Ground Event Center, 912 E Main St, Battle Ground, WA**.

Enjoy vendors, community resources, a lip-sync battle, a clothing closet, a sensory room, and a silent auction. Fill out a Passport to Pride at the event for a chance to win prizes. KN95 or better respirators/masks are required indoors, encouraged outside, and will be provided at the door while supplies last. Visit the link below for more information.
