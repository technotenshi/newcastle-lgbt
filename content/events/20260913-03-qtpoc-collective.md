QTPOC Collective is a monthly community gathering from Pride Across the Bridge and Eastside For All for 2SLGBTQIA+ Eastsiders who identify as Black, Indigenous, or People of Color. Each meetup features a different creative activity and snacks in a relaxed, community-centered space. This session meets Sunday, September 13, from 1:00–3:00pm at Bellevue Downtown Park. The meeting location rotates monthly, so see the link below for future gatherings.
