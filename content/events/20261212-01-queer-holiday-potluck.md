Pride Across the Bridge hosts its sixth annual Queer Holiday Potluck, a free, all-ages celebration for the Eastside 2SLGBTQIA+ community with food, music, and festive Sugar Plum Faerie decor. It takes place Saturday, December 12, 2026, from 6:00–8:00 PM at the Peter Kirk Community Center, 352 Kirkland Ave in Kirkland. Guests are welcome to bring dishes or drinks, and there is a sensory room and a game room for anyone who wants a calmer or more playful space. Please RSVP using the link below.
