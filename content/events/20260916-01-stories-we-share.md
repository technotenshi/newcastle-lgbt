**Stories We Share: The Courage to Connect**, hosted by the City of Redmond, invites community members to share personal stories of connection and belonging on **Wednesday, September 16, 2026, from 5:00–7:00 PM** at **The Together Center, 16305 NE 87th St, Redmond, WA**. The free event is part of Redmond's Welcoming Week. For more information, visit the link below.
