**Concerts in the Park** continues with **Brims and Beats Night** featuring the band Global Heat, on **Wednesday, August 5, 2026, from 6:00–8:30 PM** at **Lake Boren Park, 13058 SE 84th Way, Newcastle, WA**.

The park opens at 6 PM with food vendors, family activities, and community booths, followed by live music on stage at 7 PM. Dust off your funkiest hat and head to the lawn for hip-hop, funk, and feel-good rhythms. Food vendors include Bhukkad, DIY Tea Lab, L'Italiano, and Tastyz.
