Beyond Binary Connections is a free, no-expectations social space from Pride Across the Bridge for the transgender and nonbinary community. Come chat over coffee or tea, bring a book or an art project, or just hang out and play a game with other TGNB folks. This session meets Thursday, September 10, from 7:00–8:30pm at a rotating venue; see the link below for the location. The space is led by Milo, and masks are encouraged to keep the community safe.
