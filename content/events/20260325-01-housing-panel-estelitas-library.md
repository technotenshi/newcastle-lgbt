The **Washington State LGBTQ Commission** and **Estelita's Library** are co-hosting a 2SLGBTQIA+ Housing Panel, an evening of conversation with state, county, and community leaders about accessible housing initiatives and solutions for the 2SLGBTQIA+ community.

Join us on **Wednesday, March 25, 2026, at 6:00 PM** at **Estelita's Library, 2901 17th Ave S, Seattle, WA 98144** (Beacon Hill location, directly across from the Beacon Hill branch of the Seattle Public Library and a few blocks from the Beacon Hill Light Rail Station).

This event will also be available via Zoom, the link will be shared on [lgbtq.wa.gov](https://lgbtq.wa.gov/) prior to the event.

For questions, contact Commissioner **Darlin Blanco Lozano** at Darlin.Lozano@lgbtq.wa.gov.
