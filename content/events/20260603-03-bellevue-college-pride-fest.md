**Bellevue College Pride Fest** is a free outdoor celebration presented by Bellevue College's Queer and Trans Resource Center, taking place across the Theatre, Fountain, and Student Union Courtyards. Join the festivities on **Wednesday, June 3, 2026, from 11:00 AM–2:00 PM** at **Bellevue College, 3000 Landerholm Cir SE, Bellevue, WA**.

Eastside Pride will have a booth at the event — use the link above to sign up as a volunteer. For general event information, visit bellevuecollege.edu/mcs/lgbtq.
