The **HearMeWA Youth Advisory Group** is recruiting members! Open to youth aged **12–25** in Washington State, this program offers leadership opportunities, advocacy training, and a chance to shape impactful initiatives. Applications close **January 1, 2025**.

Apply now and help make a difference in your community! For more information, visit [HearMeWA.org](https://hearmewa.org/).
