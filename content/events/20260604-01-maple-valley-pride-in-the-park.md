**Maple Valley Pride in the Park** is a free, all-ages outdoor pride celebration at **Lake Wilderness Park, 22500 SE 248th St, Maple Valley, WA**, on **Thursday, June 4, 2026, from 6:00–8:00 PM**.

This year's theme is "There's Gnome Place Like Home." The event features a flag raising, food trucks, live music, resource booths, special guests, swag and giveaways, and fun activities for all ages. Eastside Pride will have a booth: use the link above to sign up as a volunteer.
