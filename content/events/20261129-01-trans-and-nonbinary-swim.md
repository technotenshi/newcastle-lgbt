Trans and Nonbinary Swim is a free, private pool night from Pride Across the Bridge for the 2SLGBTQIA+ community, with a special focus on trans and nonbinary swimmers. This month's swim is Sunday, November 29, 2026, from 4:15–7:00 PM at the Bellevue Aquatic Center, with gender-neutral locker rooms and restrooms, free snacks and drinks, and all ages welcome. Attendees under 18 must be accompanied by a responsible adult. Please RSVP using the link below.
