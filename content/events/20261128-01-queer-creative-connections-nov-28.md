Queer Creative Connections is a low-pressure monthly art meetup from Pride Across the Bridge for 2SLGBTQIA+ artists and creators to work on their own projects, collaborate, trade materials, or just spend time with fellow creatives. It meets Saturday, November 28, 2026, from 2:00–4:00 PM in Room 1 in the foyer of the Bellevue Library, 1111 110th Avenue NE, and repeats on the fourth Saturday of each month. The space is hosted by Uncle Jack of Wolfberry Studio and volunteer Robbin, and the link below has more information.
