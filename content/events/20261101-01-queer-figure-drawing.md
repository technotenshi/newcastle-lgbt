Pride Across the Bridge hosts Queer Figure Drawing, a free, laid-back clothed figure drawing session where 2SLGBTQIA+ artists of all levels take turns posing for each other. The next session is Sunday, November 1, 2026, from 2:00–4:00 PM at the Together Center, 16305 NE 87th St, Suite 110 in Redmond, and it meets on the first and third Sundays of each month. Bring your own drawing supplies, as no instruction is provided, and use the back entrance on the south side of the building. See the link below for more details.
