GenX Queer Coworking is a laid-back meetup from Pride Across the Bridge where queer Gen Xers, and anyone of any generation who wants to join, can work, study, or tackle a project alongside warm, affirming company. This session is Friday, November 20, 2026, from 1:30–4:00 PM in the small conference room at the Redmond Library. Afterward, stay for an intentionally not-working activity such as a trail walk, coffee, or ice cream. No refreshments are provided, but you can bring closed drinks or dry snacks, and the link below has more information.
