**Put your 90s pop culture knowledge to the test** at this trivia night covering music, trends, major events, and television and movies, hosted by Raised by TV.

Join us on **Tuesday, March 31, 2026, from 6:00–7:30 PM** at the Newcastle Library.

This event is open to adults ages 18 and older. Winning teams will score prizes and bragging rights, so bring a friend!

Space is limited, so please register in advance. Co-sponsored by the Friends of the Newcastle Library.
