Come celebrate as a community as the City of Newcastle Community Activities Commission raises the pride flag at City Hall in support of the LGBTQIA community.

The ceremony takes place on **Monday, June 2, 2026, at 6:00 PM** at Newcastle City Hall.

For more information, visit the City of Newcastle at newcastlewa.gov.
