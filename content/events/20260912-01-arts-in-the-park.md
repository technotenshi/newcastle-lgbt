**Arts in the Park**, hosted by OneRedmond as part of the city's Welcoming Week, brings live music and community art activities to **Downtown Park** on **Saturday, September 12, 2026, from 4:00–8:00 PM**. The free, family-friendly event celebrates the diverse backgrounds and stories that make Redmond a welcoming community. For more information, visit the link below.
