**Eastside Pride PNW** invites you to a spring social gathering, an evening of connection, celebration, and community.

Join us on **Thursday, April 2, 2026, from 6:00–8:00 PM** at **Moss Bay Hall, 111 Central Way, Kirkland, WA 98033**.

LGBTQIA+ community members and allies of all ages are welcome. The evening includes a reading of the **Kirkland City Council's Transgender Day of Visibility Proclamation**, along with heavy hors d'oeuvres and one complimentary beverage.

Registration is required. Reserve your spot at the link above.
