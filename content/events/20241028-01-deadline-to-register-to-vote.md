The deadline to register to vote for the November 5th, 2024 election is fast approaching! Make sure to register or update your address by Monday, October 28, 2024, to ensure your voice is heard. You can register online, by mail, or in person.

Don't wait—register today at Washington’s election website. Voting early is encouraged, so make a plan to vote as soon as possible!
