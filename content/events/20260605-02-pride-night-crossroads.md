**Pride Night at Crossroads Community Center** is a free, all-ages drop-in event celebrating Crossroads' first-ever Pride Night, taking place on **Friday, June 5, 2026, from 6:00–8:00 PM** at **Crossroads Community Center, 16000 NE 10th St, Bellevue, WA**.

Celebrate, connect, and build community through bracelet-making, face painting with RainbowLand, sidewalk chalk art, and more fun activities for all ages. Whether you're part of the LGBTQ+ community or an ally, everyone is welcome. Eastside Pride will have a booth: use the link above to sign up as a volunteer.
