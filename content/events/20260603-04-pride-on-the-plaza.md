**Pride on the Plaza** is a free, all-ages outdoor celebration co-hosted by the City of Bellevue and Pride Across the Bridge, centered on this year's theme "Together in Pride." The event takes place on **Wednesday, June 3, 2026, from 3:00–6:00 PM** at **Bellevue City Hall Plaza, 450 110th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA**.

The afternoon features a keynote address from Nicky Heavy Runner Chabot, remarks from city leadership, a pride flag raising, live performances, music, food, and local vendors. Eastside Pride will have a booth at the event: use the link above to sign up as a volunteer.
