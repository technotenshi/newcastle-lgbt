**Promenade Through the Park** is a free, all-ages pride celebration hosted by Redmond Parks and Recreation at the **Farrel-McWhirter Park Barnyard, 19545 NE Redmond Rd, Redmond, WA**, on **Saturday, June 6, 2026, from 2:00–3:30 PM**.

Promenade through the forest, enjoy farm animal visits, and take part in craft activities. Wear anything with rainbows or your favorite pride flag and get a special treat! Note: no pets please, as farm animals will be joining the event. Eastside Pride will have a booth: [sign up to volunteer](https://www.signupgenius.com/go/10C0944A8A72CA7FEC34-64194732-promenade#/).
