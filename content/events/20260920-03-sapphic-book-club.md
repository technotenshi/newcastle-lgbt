Pride Across the Bridge and Redmond Library have teamed up for a sapphic-centered book club for queer adults, covering everything from queer classics to messy drama to soft sapphic stories. The group meets the third Sunday of every month, 2:00–3:00pm, at Redmond Library, with this session on September 20. It's a joyful, cozy, community-rooted space open to all adults; see the link below for more information.
