Newcastle's recent decision to not raise the Pride flag at City Hall is a clear message of exclusion and intolerance. This choice undermines the values of diversity, acceptance, and community that we hold dear. Our mission is to show Mayor Clark and the councilmembers who voted against this symbolic gesture that their decision does not reflect the inclusive spirit of Newcastle.

We urge you to stand with us by signing our petition and joining our efforts to promote LGBTQ+ visibility and equality. Together, we can make it clear that Newcastle is a place where everyone, regardless of their sexual orientation or gender identity, is valued and respected. Your support is crucial in sending a powerful message that love and acceptance will always triumph over hate and ignorance. Sign the petition and join us in making a difference.
