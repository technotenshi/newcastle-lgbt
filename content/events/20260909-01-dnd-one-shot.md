Dungeon Master James hosts a drop-in one-shot Dungeons & Dragons session for LGBTQ+ players of every experience level, with dice, example character sheets, and miniatures provided. Sessions run every other Wednesday, 4:00–6:00pm, at Together Center in Redmond, with the next one on September 9. Bring a pencil and a can-do attitude; anyone under 18 must attend with an adult or legal guardian. To help James prepare for the table, message @Unevenbutter on Discord or email ahead of time, and see the link below for more details.
