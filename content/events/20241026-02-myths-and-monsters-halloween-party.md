Have a monstrously good time with other LGBTQ+ youth, ages 13-22, at **Myths & Monsters**, an LGBTQ-youth planned Halloween Party on **Saturday, October 26th, 6-9:30 p.m.** We'll be at **All Pilgrims, 500 Broadway E, Seattle, WA 98102**, with food, soft drinks, a great DJ, a quiet room with activities, friend-making, drag performances, and more! This is a safe space for LGBTQ+ youth and allies to have fun and connect.
