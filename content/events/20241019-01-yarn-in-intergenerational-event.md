Join Eastside Pride PNW for their first-ever **Yarn IN**! This intergenerational event invites LGBTQIA+ beginner knitters, crocheters, and amigurumi makers to learn new skills and have fun. Supplies will be provided—just bring your excitement and patience to learn something new this fall.

The event will be held at the Woodinville Library on Saturday, October 19, from 1 pm to 3 pm. Snacks will be available, but remember to bring your own water bottle. The class is limited to 18 attendees, so sign up early to reserve your spot!
