**Concerts in the Park** kicks off its 2026 season with **Rep and Roll Night** featuring the band Engage, on **Wednesday, July 29, 2026, from 6:00–8:30 PM** at **Lake Boren Park, 13058 SE 84th Way, Newcastle, WA**.

The park opens at 6 PM with food vendors, family activities, and community booths, followed by live music on stage at 7 PM. Suit up in your team's colors for a night of big anthems and crowd favorites on the lawn. Food vendors include Anthony's FINN, DIY Tea Lab, Go Go Mango, Paparepas, and Tastyz.
