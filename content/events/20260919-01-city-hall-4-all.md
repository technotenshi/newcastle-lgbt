**City Hall 4 All**, hosted by the City of Kirkland in alignment with Welcoming Week, is a free community celebration on **Saturday, September 19, 2026, from 10:00 AM–2:00 PM** at **Kirkland City Hall, 123 5th Ave, Kirkland, WA 98033**.

The event celebrates community efforts to make Kirkland a welcoming place for all, and organizers are calling for cultural performers, food vendors, and merchants to participate. Visit the link below to sign up.
