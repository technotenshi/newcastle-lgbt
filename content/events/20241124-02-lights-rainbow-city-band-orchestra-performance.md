Join us for **Lights: Illuminating Voices, Shining Moments**, a special performance by the Rainbow City Band and Orchestra on Sunday, November 24, at 2 p.m. at Benaroya Hall. This uplifting matinee concert features exciting works by diverse artists, celebrating themes of hope, visibility, and brilliance.

Experience a unique blend of music that will illuminate your soul and uplift your spirits as we head into the winter season. Don’t miss this opportunity to be part of a vibrant community event.
