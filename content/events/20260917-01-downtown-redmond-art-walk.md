The **Downtown Redmond Art Walk** invites visitors to explore art displays at **Downtown Park** and participating Downtown businesses on **Thursday, September 17, 2026, from 4:00–7:00 PM**. The free, self-guided walk is part of Redmond's Welcoming Week celebration. For more information, visit the link below.
