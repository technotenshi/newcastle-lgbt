Pride Across the Bridge and the Sno-Valley Senior Center host the Rainbow Elder Breakfast, a monthly chance for LGBTQ+ elders ages 50 and up to connect over good food and community. This month's breakfast is Saturday, November 7, 2026, from 10:30 AM–12:00 PM at The Egg & Us. Attendance is free, with food and drink available for purchase but not required, and help with a bill is available on request. To RSVP and get a text reminder with location details, text (971) 380-5873, and see the link below for more information.
