**Coal Creek Family YMCA** hosts its annual **Healthy Kids Day**, and City of Newcastle staff will be on hand to share information about city programs including composting and recycling, emergency preparedness, and waterway volunteer opportunities.

Join us on **Saturday, April 18, 2026, from 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM** at Coal Creek Family YMCA, 13750 Newcastle Golf Club Rd, Newcastle, WA.

Stop by, say hello, and learn how you can help make Newcastle a healthier place for everyone.
