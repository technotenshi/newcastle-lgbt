GenX Queer Coworking is a laid-back meetup from Pride Across the Bridge where queer Gen Xers can tackle work, school, or creative projects alongside warm, affirming company instead of alone. The group rotates between King County Library System locations on alternating Fridays, 1:30–4:00pm, with this session on September 18. Bring whatever project or reading you're working on; see the link below for the venue.
