**Eastside Pride PNW** and the **City of Kirkland's Teen Union Building (KTUB)** invite GSA and LGBTQIA+ club members to a free summit built around community, creativity, and inspiration.

Join us on **Saturday, May 23, 2026, from 12:00–3:00 PM** at **KTUB, 348 Kirkland Ave, Kirkland, WA**.

The afternoon includes:

- Free lunch
- Arts and crafts
- Games
- A panel of inspirational adult speakers from the LGBTQIA+ community

Free to all attendees. Email info@eastsidepridepnw.com for details.
