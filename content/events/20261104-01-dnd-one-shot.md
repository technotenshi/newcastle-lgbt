Dungeon Master James hosts a drop-in one-shot Dungeons & Dragons session for LGBTQ+ players of every experience level, with dice, example character sheets, and miniatures provided. The session runs Wednesday, November 4, 2026, from 4:00–6:00 PM at the Together Center, 16305 NE 87th St, Suite 110 in Redmond, and repeats every other Wednesday. Bring a pencil and a can-do attitude, and anyone under 18 must attend with an adult or legal guardian. Email james.volunteer@prideacrossthebridge.org to let James know you are coming, and see the link below for more details.
