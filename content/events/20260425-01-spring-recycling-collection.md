**The City of Newcastle** hosts a free **Spring Recycling Collection** for hard-to-recycle items that don't belong in your curbside bin, a twice-yearly, grant-funded opportunity to keep Newcastle clean and sustainable.

Join us on **Saturday, April 25, 2026, from 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM** at Hazelwood Elementary, 7100 116th Ave SE, Newcastle, WA.

Visit the City's What's New page for the full accepted items list and a downloadable flyer.
