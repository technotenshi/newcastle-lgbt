**Celebrate the vibrant traditions of Nowruz and Easter** by decorating eggs with a variety of colors and designs, presented by Peyvand NPO and sponsored by the Newcastle Arts Council, 4Culture, and the City of Newcastle.

Join us on **Saturday, April 11, 2026, from 2:00 – 3:00 PM** at KCLS Newcastle Library, Newcastle, WA.

All supplies are included and registration is not required; event passes are distributed starting 30 minutes before the program, limited to 30 participants.
