Join **Congresswoman Jayapal** for a **Community Forum** on **February 18, 2025**, from **6:00–7:30 PM** at **Town Hall Seattle**. This event is at capacity, but you can join the waitlist for a chance to attend. Hear about her efforts in Congress, participate in a panel discussion with local leaders, and gather with others in the community to chart a path forward during these challenging times.

Don't forget to arrive early to check out booths from community partners serving the district!
