Join us for an exciting **LGBTQ+ Game Night** featuring **Bracketeering & Jackbox Games**! This virtual event is happening on **Saturday, March 8, 2025, from 7:00 – 8:30 PM** on **Zoom**. Open to LGBTQ+ youth ages **11–22**, this is a fun and inclusive space to connect, compete, and share laughs over a game of strong opinions about silly topics!  

For event details and Zoom access, email **[kcgroups@lamberthouse.org](mailto:kcgroups@lamberthouse.org)**. Don’t miss out on a night of community, fun, and friendly competition!
