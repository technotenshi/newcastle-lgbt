**Africans on the Eastside**, **The Circle**, and **Eastside For All** host **Eastside Immigrant Youth Advocacy Day** on **Sunday, September 20, 2026, from 1:00–5:00 PM** at **The Together Center, 16305 NE 87th St, Redmond, WA**. The free event closes out Redmond's Welcoming Week with workshops and advocacy resources for immigrant youth. For more information, visit the link below.
