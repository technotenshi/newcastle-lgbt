The **Name & Gender Marker Change Legal Clinic** is happening on **February 23, 2025**, from **1–4 PM** at the **PFLAG Columbia Branch, Seattle Public Library**. If you need help with updating your name or gender marker, volunteers including lawyers, paralegals, case workers, and social workers will be available to assist you. Notaries will also be on-site for any necessary notarizations.

To register, fill out the form [here](https://forms.gle/iKDj794Xp43yA2UP7).
