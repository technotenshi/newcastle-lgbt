Capital City Pride presents Murder and a Show II: The Premiere, a drag cabaret dinner theater murder mystery featuring Lucy Elle Balls at Wild Child in Olympia. The evening premieres "Stilettos in the Woods," a film unraveling the story behind a local drag performer's on-screen murder, on Friday, October 30, 2026, from 6:00–9:30 PM. Tickets are $20 during presale through September 30 and $25 starting in October, and the event is black tie, with Halloween costumes welcomed as haute couture.
