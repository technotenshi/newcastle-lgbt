Eastside Pride PNW invites knitters and crocheters to craft 7x9-inch squares for Warm Up America, donated to help keep people warm through the colder months ahead. The event runs from noon–3:30 PM on Sunday, September 13, 2026, at the Friends of Youth Main Office, 13116 NE 132nd Street in Kirkland. Snacks and beverages will be provided, and this is a crafting session rather than a lesson or ongoing knitting club, so bring your own skills and supplies. Register using the link below.
