Pride Across the Bridge and the Redmond Library co-host EnGAYging with Sapphistry, a sapphic-centered book club for queer adults who love queer classics, messy drama, or soft sapphic stories. It meets Sunday, November 15, 2026, from 2:00–3:00 PM at the Redmond Library, 15990 NE 85th St, and repeats on the third Sunday of each month. The event is free for ages 18 and up with no registration required, and tea is provided, so bring your own mug. Questions can go to Noel at nsreich@kcls.org, and the link below has more details.
