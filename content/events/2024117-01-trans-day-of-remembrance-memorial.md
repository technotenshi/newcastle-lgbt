Save the date! We are planning a **Trans Day of Remembrance** memorial service on the afternoon of **Sunday, November 17**, in downtown **Bellevue**. Whatever the weeks ahead may hold, this is a time to find caring community and to remember. Hosted by **PFLAG Bellevue/Eastside** and **Emerald City Social Club** at **Bellevue First United Methodist Church**.
