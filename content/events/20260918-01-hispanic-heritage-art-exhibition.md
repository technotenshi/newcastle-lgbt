**Centro Cultural Mexicano** presents a **Hispanic Heritage Month Art Exhibition** on **Friday, September 18, 2026, from 9:00 AM–7:00 PM** at **Centro Cultural Mexicano, 16300 Redmond Way Ste 100, Redmond, WA**. The free exhibition, part of Redmond's Welcoming Week, showcases work celebrating Hispanic heritage and culture. For more information, visit the link below.
