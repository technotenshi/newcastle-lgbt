Lambert House's monthly outdoor outing for LGBTQ+ youth explores the Snoqualmie Tunnel, a 2.3-mile-long tunnel that makes a fitting Halloween Day hike. The group meets at Lambert House in Seattle at 9:30 AM on Saturday, October 31, 2026, and returns by 2:45 PM, leaving time to get ready for trick-or-treating.
