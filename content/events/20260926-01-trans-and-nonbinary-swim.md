Trans and Nonbinary Swim, also known as Splash of Pride, is a free, private pool night from Pride Across the Bridge for the 2SLGBTQIA+ community, with a special focus on trans and nonbinary swimmers. It rotates monthly between pools in Redmond, Bellevue, Issaquah, Kirkland, and North Bend, on the fourth weekend of the month. This session is Saturday, September 26, from 4:15–7:00pm; see the link below for this month's location and to RSVP.
