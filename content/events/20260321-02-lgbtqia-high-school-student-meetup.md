**Eastside Pride PNW** and the **Kirkland Arts Center** are hosting a free meet-up for LGBTQIA+ high school students, come create art, build friendships, and make unforgettable memories.

Join us on **Saturday, March 21, 2026, from 3:00–5:00 PM** at **The Gallery at KAC, Kirkland Arts Center, 620 Market St, Kirkland, WA**.

Snacks and drinks will be provided. Admission is **free** and no registration is required.

For questions, contact **Eastside Pride PNW** at info@eastsidepridepnw.com.
