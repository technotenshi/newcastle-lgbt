Bring your pup and your pride! The **Bellevue Downtown Association** and **Eastside Pride PNW** present the **Paws & Pride Dog Walk**, a celebration of community, dogs, and LGBTQ+ joy in the heart of Bellevue.

Join us on **Saturday, June 6, 2026, from 10:00 AM–2:00 PM** at **Bellevue Downtown Park, 10201 NE 4th St, Bellevue, WA**.

Register at the link above to secure your spot.

Want to be more involved? **Volunteers** can sign up at [signupgenius.com](https://www.signupgenius.com/go/10C0944A8A72CA7FEC34-62232557-paws#/). LGBTQ+ **stage performers and vendors** interested in participating can reach out to info@eastsidepridepnw.com.
