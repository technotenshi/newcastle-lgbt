Beyond Binary Connections is a no-expectations social space from Pride Across the Bridge for the transgender and nonbinary community, with board and card games, chill conversation, and room to bring a book or art project. This session meets Thursday, November 12, 2026, from 7:00–8:30 PM at the Woodinville Panera Bread, 13780 NE 175th St. All ages are welcome, masks are encouraged, and the space is led by Milo.
