Join the Queer Teen Book Club to discuss *The Passing Playbook* by Isaac Fitzsimons! This event offers a space to connect with other 2SLGBTQIA+ teens and discuss meaningful topics. You can participate either in person at the Sammamish Library or online.

Snacks are provided by the Friends of the Sammamish Library for in-person attendees. If joining online, make sure to register ahead of time to receive the Zoom link. Reasonable accommodations and closed captioning are available for accessibility.
