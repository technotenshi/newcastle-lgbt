**Pride Mugs on the Wheel** is a pottery wheel class where you'll throw and decorate your very own mug using the pride colors of your choosing. The class takes place on **Monday, June 1, 2026, from 7:00–9:30 PM** at the **Northwest Pottery Collective, 1445 Northwest Mall Street, Issaquah, WA**.

No experience is necessary and the class is open to ages 16 and up. Children ages 10–15 are welcome to enroll alongside an enrolled adult. Register via the link above to reserve your spot.
