**Garage Pride** is a free outdoor pride event hosted by The Garage Teen Cafe and the City of Issaquah, taking place on **Friday, June 5, 2026, from 3:00–6:00 PM** on the **Issaquah Community Center Lawn, 301 Rainier Blvd S, Issaquah, WA**.

Come out for games, crafts, and freebies, plus a taco truck on site (food available for purchase). Visit the link above for more information.
