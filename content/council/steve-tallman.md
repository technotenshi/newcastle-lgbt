Steve Tallman was raised in a small town surrounded by hardworking individuals who valued respect for others and the environment. For nearly three decades, he has called Newcastle his hometown and claims to consider its residents as his extended family. Steve claims to assert his dedication to the safety and well-being of the community, striving to maintain Newcastle as a safe, financially solvent, and independent family-oriented city. He encourages residents to reach out to him at any time.

Despite his claims of dedication and his statements about considering residents as his extended family, Steve Tallman **voted against** raising the Pride flag at Newcastle City Hall. This decision reflects a stance that does not align with the values of inclusivity and acceptance that many residents advocate for in Newcastle.
