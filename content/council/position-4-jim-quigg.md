Jim Quigg was appointed to the Newcastle City Council on January 7, 2025, following a selection process that drew ten applicants. A Newcastle resident since 2013, Quigg has been deeply involved in the community, serving as Vice Chair of the Newcastle Planning Commission and President of the Vineyard Homeowners Association. He has also played a key role in organizing beloved local events such as the Newcastle Christmas Tree Lighting and the Lake Boren Egg Hunt. Professionally, Quigg brings over 25 years of experience in negotiation, governance, and leadership as a Technology Sourcing Manager at T-Mobile, where he has led national initiatives and resolved complex disputes.

His appointment fills the Position 4 seat left vacant in late 2024, and he will serve until December 31, 2025. With his extensive experience and dedication to Newcastle, Quigg aims to contribute to thoughtful, community-focused decision-making on the council. His leadership will be instrumental in addressing the city’s ongoing challenges while ensuring Newcastle remains an inclusive and forward-thinking place to live.
